# Problem 14: troops could not be moved between kingdoms, and nothing stored what a transfer needs

## The problem (packets)

Moving troops to another kingdom (for instance to bring Fire Peaks castles some knights) could only be done in
the game client. The packets were not documented, and two guesses in the notes were wrong:

* `msk` was thought to skip a map march or a robber baron cooldown. It carries no march or tower id; it spends a
  **minute-skip item** (`MST` `MS1` to `MS7`, 1 min to 24 h) on a **kingdom transfer**, `KID` being the transfer's
  *target* kingdom and `TT` the transfer type (`"1"` troops).
* "kid 2 (sands)" in the `kut` note: the packet says `SKID` 1, and the account's Sands castle is in kingdom 1.

The price was unknown too. The client code (`CastleTransferTroopsDialog.travelCostC1`) gave
`round(sum(kingdomTravellingCost * count) * unitTravelTaxRate(target) / 100)`, with the unit cost on each unit and the tax
on the **target** kingdom in the items file. It matched the observed 210-coin drop for one unit 277 into green.

All of this was then proven live on 2026-10-17 by injecting through `proxy_packet_monitor`: 20 Travelling Knights
(unit 655) from green to Fire Peaks charged exactly 2,500 coins, two `MS5` skips landed the 7200 s journey, and `dcl`
showed the knights in the Fire Peaks castle. Details: [`../game_infrastructure/kingdom_transfer_rules.md`](../game_infrastructure/kingdom_transfer_rules.md).

## The problem (database and state)

* The direct session ignored `gcu` (coins), `kpi` (kingdoms and transfers) and `sce` (skip stock), even though `gbd`
  carries all three at login. Nothing could check coins, an unlocked kingdom or a transfer already travelling.
* A `kpi` is a **snapshot**: the client clears its transfer list on every one. Merging it, or treating "no `UT`" as
  "unknown", would never notice a landing.
* A castle's stock changes the moment a `kut` leaves, so a reading older than the last send would let a second
  transfer exceed what the castle holds.
* A transfer in doubt (no reply) must never be sent again, and a request left queued must not fire hours later when
  the bot is stopped.

## The fix

* `empire-core/src/transfer.rs` (rules, pure): order-exact `kut` / `msk` / `kpi` packets, parsers for `kpi`, `gcu`,
  `sce` and the `kut` reply, the cost, and `check`, which names every reason a transfer would be refused (stock
  read after the last send, coins, unlocked kingdom, one transfer per target, bot off, castle not out as support,
  skip item and stock). Unknown is never a pass.
* `empire-game/src/generated/travel_costs.rs`, generated by `utils/scripts/gen_travel_costs.py` from the items file (both since replaced by `utils/scripts/build_tables.py` and `generated/units.rs`/`rules.rs`, see [`16.md`](16.md)):
  344 unit costs, each kingdom's tax and journey time, the seven skip items.
* Migration **V26**: `kingdom_transfer` jobs; coins, kingdom state and skip stock as replace-whole snapshots in
  `app_state`, filled from every packet that carries them (`direct/transfer.rs`, `observe`), `gbd` included.
* `direct/transfer.rs`: one transfer at a time, only while the bot is off, each pause drawn from the existing wave
  generator (`Wait::Transfer`, 1 to 12 s), checks repeated right before the `kut`, a reply that never comes
  reconciled with a `kpi` read (never a second `kut`), two refusals in ten minutes pause sending, a queued request
  expires after five minutes.
* `/v1/transfers` API (`API_VERSION` 54) and the Castle Overview widget (`views/transfer.js`), a projection of the
  API that submits intent and computes nothing.

Design: [`../architecture/kingdom-transfer.md`](../architecture/kingdom-transfer.md).

## Found on the way

`proxy_packet_monitor` commits an active recording to disk only every 100 events, so replies cannot be read live from
a recording in progress. Stopping the recording flushes and finalises it; stop, read, start was used for the live proof.
(Noted in that tool's README.)

## Not verified

* **The new code has not sent anything to the live server.** The packets and numbers are the ones proven by hand; the
  runner is tested on a fake clock with those exact packets and replies, and the API handlers are tested directly.
* Only `MS5` skips, only `CID` -1, only `TT` "1", and only two target kingdoms (green, Fire Peaks) were sent. The
  error statuses of `kut` / `msk` are not known.
* Whether a second transfer to a kingdom already being travelled to is allowed is unknown, so it is refused.
* The widget was built and unit-tested but not looked at in the running app.
