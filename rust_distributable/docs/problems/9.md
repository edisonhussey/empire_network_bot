# Problem 9: robber barons recorded as PVP, and a spotlight that would not settle

## 1. NPC movements in the PVP record (packets and database)

The PVP tab recorded every `gam` movement that was not our own outward one. A robber baron's
castle, a fortress or any other NPC is not a player: the packet gives the movement's sender (`SID`)
and a castle array (`SA`) but `O[]` holds no record for it, so the name, the alliance and the
player id are all empty. Such a movement was classified by alliance alone and so landed in the
table as an `incoming` or `other` row with no name. Worse, our own army coming home from an NPC
castle is also a `gam` movement, from that castle to ours, and it was recorded as an attack on us.

**Fix.** `is_player_movement` (`empire-core/src/pvp.rs`): the sender needs a player record; the
target does too, unless it is one of our castles. Anything else is NPC traffic and `classify`
returns `None`, so it is never written. Rows already in `pvp_movement` are hidden by the same rule
when read (`PLAYER_ONLY` in `store/pvp.rs`), in the list and in the counts, so no migration was
needed and nothing is deleted. A movement first seen without its player records is simply not
recorded then; a later packet that has them is, since the primary key is the MID and the earlier
one left no row. Station threat detection already required both records, so the two agree.

## 2. A spotlight that followed every pick

On the 100 x 100 map the spotlight jumped about as the picks did: it was put on each accepted pick
(plus a look-ahead), and the weights at `p = 1` made far towers collectively more likely than
near ones, so picks did not group. Measured on a simulated field, it moved 61 tiles per pick, with
73-tile relocations. It now averages recent picks and drifts (1.8 tiles per pick), picks lie closer
(28 tiles against 45) and a relocation is a walk of at most 6 tiles. Details and the table:
[`../architecture/target-selection.md`](../architecture/target-selection.md). The spotlight is
still discarded when the kingdom changes.
