# Problem 5: moving an attacked castle's troops out and back (`dcl`, `sdi`, `cds`, `mcm`)

Building auto-station on the packets captured by the proxy prototype ran into seven
things the packets, the old brief and the existing bot did not make obvious. Each is
listed with what was found and how it is handled. The sequence itself is in
`docs/network/sequences/support.md` and the design in
`docs/architecture/station.md`.

## 1. The brief had `sdi` and `cds` the wrong way round

`support prompt.md` described an earlier sequence. The captures show `sdi` only *opens*
the support page (and returns the fresh home stock and the castle id) and `cds` is the
send. Building from the brief would have treated an `sdi` reply as a dispatch.

**Fix.** `sdi` is a read; only a `cds` success reply, matched to the pending castle and
destination, confirms a march. Anything else is not taken as confirmation.

## 2. Two MIDs, and one that changes

The attack's MID (what `mrm` names) is not the support march's MID (what `mcm` names), and
a MID is replaced when a march lands and turns back. A recall built from the attack's MID
would have named nothing.

**Fix.** The job keeps both: the attack MIDs it covers and the support MID read from the
`cds` reply. A recall is never sent before the support MID is known, even when a retreat
arrives first; it waits for the reply.

## 3. A five second window against a heavy-tailed timer

The bot's human-paced wait generator is low-heavy with a long tail (minutes). Spacing the
`dcl`, `sdi` and `cds` steps with raw draws would have missed the landing on any long draw.

**Fix.** Each draw is squashed monotonically into a window that fits, the gaps are scaled
down together when time is short, and optional steps are dropped in order when even the
floors do not fit (`normalise_gaps`, `plan_prep`). Verified over 72 simulated runs: the
acknowledgement came at least 3.0 s before the landing. This is a simulation with a fake
clock, not live latency.

## 4. Support takes the troops an attack is about to use

Support draws on the castle's troops. An attack handshake that had read the stock with
`adi` and then sent its `cra` after the support left would be refused (101/313), and
repeated refusals lock the account for 24 hours.

**Fix.** Three layers. While a job is in its window nothing else sends (the gate). A `cds`
waits for an unanswered `cra` from the same castle. And from the moment support leaves
until the troops are home (the recall's `TT - PT` plus a margin) the castle is held: a
pending `cra` from it is released safely, and no new attack is started from it. Verified in
`direct/tests.rs::station_holds` and `direct/station_tests.rs`.

## 5. A silent `cds` or `mcm`

A `cds` with no answer may still have worked; sending it again could duplicate the
support. A silent `mcm` may have recalled the troops; the prototype also refused to repeat
it blindly.

**Fix.** Neither is repeated. A silent `cds` makes the job *uncertain*: its commander and
its castle hold stay, and it is reconciled if the account's own movement to the destination
shows up in a `gam` (the MID is adopted and recalled). A silent `mcm` is stored as
`saved:recall_uncertain` and logged so it can be checked in game. Refusals are stored with
their status.

## 6. Borrowing a commander without costing attacks

A support march needs a commander (`LID`), and attacks have strict commander sections. The
`gli.C` roster does not say who is free.

**Fix.** The commander ledger decides: a free commander no running task uses, else the last
free one of the task with the most free ones (tasks take their first free one, so the last
are least used), else one back well before `D`. Only ids in the curated usable list are
considered. The lease is written to `commander_state` at once, revalidated just before
`cds`, and freed at once if nothing leaves.

## 7. `dcl` mixes troops and tools; the alliance field has a "none" value

`AC` in `dcl` lists troops and tools together (ladder 614, banner 660 and 637 are tools)
and zero-count rows. And `AID = -1` means no alliance, so two unaffiliated players compare
equal.

**Fix.** Only ids in the troop table are sent as support and zero counts are dropped
(`select_support_troops`, at most ten types, largest first); the Overview separates troops,
tools and unknown ids with the same tables. The trigger needs both alliance ids present and
different, so `-1` against `-1` is skipped, as the operator specified. The raw `AID` is now
kept on each side of a movement (`Party::alliance_raw`) so the rule can tell a missing record
from "no alliance".

## Database

`station_job` keeps the numbers the UI needs and a state blob, so an outstanding support
march survives a restart; `castle_inventory` keeps the latest `dcl` reading per castle;
`station_bot*` hold the destinations (migration V22). Nothing is stored that depends on
secrets.
